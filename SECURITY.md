# Security Policy

## Authorized Target

Security testing is authorized only against the dedicated bug-bounty deployment
and the Supabase project explicitly listed by the bounty program.

Production domains, production Supabase projects, third-party providers,
denial-of-service testing, social engineering, and access to real customer data
are out of scope.

## Reporting

Reports should include reproduction steps, affected role, business impact, and
the minimum proof necessary to demonstrate the issue. Do not retain or publish
test data obtained during research.
